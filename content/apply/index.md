<div class="apply-intro">

Thank you for your interest in joining the lab. Before you begin, please read the
[key information for prospective members](/application/application-undergrad/) and the
[Applicant Manual](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/lvlgxtnoffurd2fpbl3tg/Applicant-manual.pdf?rlkey=b777jxfm4ciadocgkv6xnogge&dl=0),
which explain what we do, what we expect, and how applications are evaluated.

The form takes about 30–45 minutes. Your text answers are saved in your browser as you type, so you
can leave and come back on the same device. You will need a **PDF of your résumé/CV**; an unofficial
transcript and a writing sample are optional.

**Deadline:** applications for Spring 2027 are due **November 15, 2026**. We review applications on a
rolling basis after the deadline and will respond by email within two weeks.
